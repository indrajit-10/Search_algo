The ranked cards appear here.

The catalogue is synthetic. The real card export is private, so these 12,510 cards were generated from the repository's own dictionaries: the category codes are real and parse exactly as real ones do, but card titles come from templates, and combinations like Events in April / Diwali are artefacts of random generation. Judge the mechanism, not the card names. Roughly 40% of cards carry a title that never names their occasion, which is the case keyword search cannot handle.

Ranking runs entirely in your browser and is a direct port of search/rank.py: same BM25F field weights, same coverage scaling, same common-word cutoff, same intent table, same seasonal rule, same relevance floor, same per-category cap.
