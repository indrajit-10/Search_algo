01The pipeline

Six stages, top to bottom. The loop is the important part: when too little matches, the search widens and comes back rather than giving up.

02How the search widens

Rungs are given up from the top down, least important first, collecting results as it goes. It stops as soon as the page is full.

“full” is 20 — replayed over 515 real queries

So a page is nearly always full, but rarely from one rung alone. The typical query matches 24 cards outright — just over a page — and a sixth of them match nothing until the search widens. MAX_RESULTS in search_engine.py is the single knob; the browser test bench asks for 24.

03Autocomplete

A separate path, running before anything is submitted. The list is built once when the server starts; every keystroke is a lookup against it.
